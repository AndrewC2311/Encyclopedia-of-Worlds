This expertly crafted shortsword boasts a silver-like hilt, its elegant design offering both balance and precision. The blade, forged from a pale green metal, holds an unnatural sharpness, capable of slicing with deadly ease. When evil lurks nearby, the blade shimmers with an eerie glow, as if thirsting to strike down the darkness.

## ✨ Magical Properties
* +2 Shortsword
* Glows in the presence of evil creatures (within 15')
