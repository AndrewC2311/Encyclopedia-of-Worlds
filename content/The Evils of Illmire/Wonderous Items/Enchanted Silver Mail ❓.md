A suit of shining silver chainmail, masterfully crafted with an almost otherworldly sheen. The links are impossibly smooth, glinting even in dim light. Embossed upon the chest is an unfamiliar crest—a regal gryphon poised in mid-flight, its wings outstretched as if ready to take to the skies.

## ✨ Magical Properties
* +1 Chainmail (15 AAC)
* Wearer gains +1 Charisma
