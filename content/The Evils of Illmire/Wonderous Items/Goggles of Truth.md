A pair of finely crafted lenses set in an aged brass frame, their glass faintly iridescent, catching the light with subtle hints of deep blue. The frames bear intricate etchings resembling flowing script or arcane symbols, their meaning long forgotten. When worn, the world appears unchanged at first, but there is an uncanny sense that hidden truths lie just beyond the veil of sight.

## ✨ Magical Properties
* **While Worn**: Detects magical bodily alterations and gender changes. Affected areas shimmer with a magical blue light.
