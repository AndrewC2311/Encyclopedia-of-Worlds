A miniature golden statuette depicts two lions locked in a fierce roar, their craftsmanship exquisite and lifelike. A delicate inscription in an ancient magical script adorns its base, revealing a hidden command word. When spoken, the statuette hums softly and emits a faint glow, yet its true power remains dormant—there must be another step to unlock its full potential.

## ✨ Magical Properties
* Speaking the magical command word, make the figure glow faintly for around 5 seconds.
